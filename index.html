A letter to Mama

Mama,

There is no mathematics that can measure what you have done for me. You stood alone at the crossroads where most would have buckled — and you chose me, every single day.

You were the alarm clock and the lullaby. The one who packed the lunch and wiped the tears. The one who worked when your body was tired and still saved enough warmth to make me feel safe. You wore the weight of two parents like it was the simplest thing in the world — and you made it look like love, never like sacrifice.

I see it now, Mama. I see every early morning you traded for my comfort. Every dream you quietly tucked away so mine could breathe. Every moment you were afraid but smiled for me anyway.

You are the reason I know what strength looks like. You are the reason I know that love is not just a feeling — it is a choice you make again and again, even when it is hard. Especially when it is hard.

No gift, no card, no day on the calendar can hold the size of my gratitude. But I want you to know — you are my greatest blessing, and I am so proud to be your child.

Thank you for being everything. You are, and will always be, my whole heart.

Forever yours 🌹
